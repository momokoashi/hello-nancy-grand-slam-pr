## Why it's a story

**The ambush.** Meta won't take the ads. No sports property will sell the category a sponsorship. So the brand took the fortnight anyway — New York street activity, a creator programme, and the campaign line printed on the box.

**Designed to be left out.** Every other brand solves for hiding: matte black, looks-like-a-lipstick. Hello Nancy solves for the opposite. ACE is 65 mm and 110 g because that's a real tennis ball. On a shelf, it simply is one.

**A million, with no ads.** Zero to a million-plus customers in three years, in a category locked out of paid media. The growth engine is the packaging and word of mouth — which makes it a business story, not just a product one.
