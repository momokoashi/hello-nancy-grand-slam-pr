## Feature badges as printed on the box

Deep & Rumbly Vibrations · Flexible Head · Whisper Quiet · Fully Waterproof · USB-C Rechargeable · Body-Safe Silicone · 12 Intensities & Patterns

> ⚠️ **Internal note — do not send to press.** The box prints *12 Intensities & Patterns*; the engineering spec says *8 modes × 5 intensities* (40 combinations). These are not the same claim and a reviewer with the product in hand will count. Resolve before this sheet is distributed — either the artwork changes or the press language does. See `06_tricky-questions-and-fact-check-pack.md` §C.
>
> Until resolved, all press materials should say **"eight modes across five intensities"** — it is defensible against the product in hand and it is a *bigger* number than twelve, not a smaller one.
